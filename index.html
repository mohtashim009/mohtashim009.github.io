Project 02 · Computer Vision · Deep Learning

Fruit Quality Detection

97.18% validation accuracy · 95.58% macro F1

A Flask application that identifies a fruit or vegetable and classifies it as Fresh or Rotten. I owned the full ML workflow: dataset preparation, transfer learning, fine-tuning, evaluation, error analysis, and inference integration.

  • →Trained on 29,291 images across 28 classes covering 14 fruit/vegetable types and fresh/rotten labels.
  • →Started with a frozen MobileNetV2 backbone, then fine-tuned the last 30 layers at a low learning rate of 1e-5.
  • →Went beyond accuracy with per-class precision/recall/F1 and error analysis to identify difficult classes and common confusions.
